### Stage 1 — Read and map the field

Use:

```text
/research-wiki init
/research-lit "topic" — sources: web, arxiv, zotero, obsidian
/research-review "research question and candidate method"
```

Record useful papers and rejected directions in the wiki. Distinguish `paper says` from `we infer`. For paper notes, use the existing `paper-reading` / `paper-reader` skills when a full local paper must be read.

Exit gate: the gap, closest prior work, and proposed claim are written in `PAPER_BRIEF.md` and `CLAIMS_REGISTER.md`.
